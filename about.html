ABOUT ME

JAMIE HWANG-BLACK

Hello! I'm Jamie, a queer multidisciplinary artist based in Chicago.

I was born and raised in Daejeon, South Korea. After studying English Language and Literature at Ewha Womans University, I moved to Chicago with my wife in 2024 and continued my education at Harold Washington College.

The Printmaking department at HWC was a catalyst for my artistic growth. After earning my Associate of Science degree in 2026, I am now pursuing a degree in Computer Science and Design at the University of Illinois Chicago.

For collaborations, commissions, and purchase inquiries, please don't hesitate to contact me at: jamiehwangblack@gmail.com
