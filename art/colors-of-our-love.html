The Colors Of Our Love


Collage, 2023

“The Colors Of Our Love” is a collage exploring sapphic sexuality, reclaiming imagery that often commodifies femininity and redirecting it toward intimacy and pleasure. It evokes sweetness and sensual vulnerability without relying on explicit depiction, resisting fixed narratives shaped by the male gaze. Instead, it presents sapphic desire as playful, intimate, and self-defined. Through the reassembly of sexual elements like ripe fruit, makeup, and lingerie, the work asserts the body as a site of agency where queerness is not hidden, but shared and tasted. Commissioned in 2023 as the cover for the annual zine by the LGBTQ+ club at Ewha Woman’s University, the piece was ultimately deemed “problematic and explicit” by senior members of the club and not permitted to be published.
