Small Cat Big City

Mixed Media, 2026

This piece depicts our cat, Jasmine, who we got soon after moving into Marina City.

I always wondered what she could possibly make of the view from our balcony - whether or not she understood that we were high up, that the little moving specks far below were people. Before we moved out, I wanted to create a snapshot of the time she was a kitten growing up in this beautiful and confusing space.
