Silkscreen, 2026

This piece was created using four different stencils burned into a silkscreen, printed in the order of Yellow, Red, Blue, and Grey. It serves as a self portrait without the self, combining visually and sentimentally significant architectural structures throughout different stages my life that took place in the cities of Daejeon, Seoul, and Chicago. I arranged these symbols into a composition in the shape of a human heart. Marina City, my most recent and emotionally definitive home, stands in place of the aorta - the largest artery that transports oxygen directly from the heart to the rest of the body.

The printing process was refined through much experimentation with CMYK layering. After trying out many different combinations, I ultimately decided to use a mix of translucent and opaque screenprinting ink.

Using a combination of Procreate and GIMP, I created four iterations of this file, each filling in different areas for Cyan, Yellow, Magenta, and Gray.

Printing this piece was quite physically exhausting, especially as I was dealing with flare-ups of my chronic pain condition. During this process, I became much more familiar with switching between digital and traditional tools to maximize creative freedom and flexibility. I also learnt a lesson in the importance of patience in art-making.
