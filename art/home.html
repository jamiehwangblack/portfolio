Silkscreen, 2026

This piece was created using four different stencils burned into a silkscreen, printed in the order of Yellow, Red, Blue, and Grey. The printing process was refined through much experimentation with CMYK layering using a mix of translucent and opaque screenprinting ink. It serves as a self portrait without the self, combining visually and sentimentally significant architectural structures throughout different stages my life that took place in the cities of Daejeon, Seoul, and Chicago. These symbols are arranged into a composition taking the shape of a human heart. Marina City, my most recent and emotionally definitive home, stands in place of the aorta - the largest artery that transports oxygen directly from the heart to the rest of the body.
