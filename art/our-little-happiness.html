Our Little Happiness


Zinc Plate Etching, 2026

This piece was created on a zinc plate through five iterations of etching with added layers of aquatint throughout the course of several weeks. It serves to memorialize the space that my spouse and I lived in, from the beginning of our life in Chicago to the end of our first year of marriage. It depicts the interior of our apartment reflecting onto the view from the balcony outside, combining them into a cohesive memory of the happiness we felt in this space. The light shining from the kitchen envelops our figure in a bright spotlight, creating vivid contrast and visualizing the feeling of being the main characters in the climax of a romantic film. The Korean text on the top and bottom translates to: "May our little happiness shine brightly upon the world".
