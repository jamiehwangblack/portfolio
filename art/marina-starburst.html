Marina Starburst


Mixed Media, 2026

This artwork was created during my last month of living in Marina City as a farewell gift to Kenya, who worked at the mailroom in the building. At the core of this piece is a sense of awe at the architectural beauty of the corn-cobs. I find myself often returning to the imagery of a burst of light, or a "starburst". Starbursts are prominent in my field of vision as I have Visual Snow Syndrome, a chronic neurological condition that affects the vision in various ways. In this piece, I use it to express the overwhelming joy and sorrow that I will always feel looking back at my memories in these towers.
